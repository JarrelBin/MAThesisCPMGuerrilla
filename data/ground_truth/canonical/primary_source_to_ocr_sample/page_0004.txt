BRIEF HISTORY BACKGROUND
3. Subject was born in 1963 at SAMNAK OK and the 2nd eldest child in the family of five children. Her parents were padi planters cum rubber tappers. When subject was 10 years old, she was admitted to Standard One in BAN SAMNAK OK Thai School and passed Standard Three at the age of 13 years. Owing to poverty, she stopped schooling and worked as a rubber tapper at SAMNAK OK.
CIRCUMSTANCES LEADING TO JOINING CTO
4. While subject was tapping rubber in SAMNAK OK in 1978, CT MAHAROM (f) (8) and CT NAZIMAH (f) (9) used to visit subject at her place of work and propagated the luxurious life in CTO to her. About 3 months later, the CTs invited subject to visit their camp. About one month later, subject accepted their invitation and was brought to the HQ camp of Area 'E' Special District in SAMNAK OK. The journey took 3 days. Upon arrival at the HQ camp of Area 'E' Special District, a tea party was held and a welcoming speech was given by CT MUDA (23), the leader of Area 'E' Special District. A few days later, subject was brought to the HQ camp of Special District in BAN BALA, escorted by 4 CTs. The journey from the HQ camp of Area 'E' Special Distriot to HQ camp of Special District took about 6 days. Upon arrival at the HQ camp of Special District, subject was welcome by CT AH KOK (10), who gave a welcoming speech. Subsequently a tea party was held.
TRAINING
5. Five days after arrival at the HQ camp Special District in BAN BALA, subject underwent military training as well as attended political class together with
CT LIJAH (f) (11). CT SAMSUDIN (12) was responsible for military training and CT HALIM (13) for political class. The training lasted for 6 months.
6. After having completed the training, subject was assigned for sentry and odd job duties. Occasionally, subject was assigned for food transportation duty at half way between HQ camp of Special District in BAN BALA and BAN JEH NAI. Usually, she went with CT LIJAH (f) (11), CT FARIDAH (f) (14) and CT AMINAH (f) (15).
CT AYAH (16) - NEW LEADER OF
SPECIAL DISTRICT
7. In AUG 81, a group of about 30 CTs came to the HQ camp of Special District. A few days later, they left the HQ camp except CT AYAH (16), CT SANAH (f) (17), CT PAK ALI
(18), CT TEKAT (19), CT BUDI (f) (20), CT PAK NGAH (21) and CT TINI (f) (22). About one week later, CT AH KOK (10) announced in the HQ camp that CT AYAH (16) had replaced him as leader of Special District and he became his deputy.
