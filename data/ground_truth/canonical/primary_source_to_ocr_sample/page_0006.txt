TENIK (26), was granted permission to visit his parents in a rubber estate in PRAKOB TOK by CT PAK NGAH (21) - the CT leader of Area 'E' Special District. Through the arrange-
ment by the masses in PRAKOB TOK, subject's husband (SEP ISMAIL TENIK (26)) met his parents on 4 APR 82 in a rubber estate in PRAKOB TOK. On 5 APR 82, at about 2000 hours,
subject and her husband dressed in civvies absconded without any weapons from the rubber estate in PRAKOB TOK and arrived at SEP ISMAIL TENIK's (26) elder sister's house in BAN KRONG at about 2300 hours. Thereafter, they were sent to SEP ISMAIL TENIK's (26) house at CHANA on two motor cycles by SEP ISMAIL TENIK's (26) brother-in-law named DAUD (32) and his friends, arriving at about 0130 hours on 6 APR 82. At about 0900 hours of the same day, SEP ISMAIL TENIK's (26) brother-in-law and friends brought them to District Officer of NATHAWI office, where they surrendered. On 7 APR 82, at about 1130 hours, they were brought to CPM 43 in PATTANI.
COMMENT
14. Subject is a Thai, aged 19 years and received three years of Thai education. She had joined CTO for about 31/2 years. During interrogation, subject was friendly, communicative and quite co-operative. A very short time of two hours were given to the interrogating officer by CPM-43 in PATTANI to interview her before her release on 21 MAY 1982. As such only a brief interview report was recorded from her. However, a more comprehensive statement has been recorded from her husband SEP ISMAIL TENIK @ ALIMAT.
